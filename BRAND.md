Unlike traditional perp DEXs that only care about collateral, Arcadia surfaces the signal — who can actually trade — and lets capital find its way to proven performers.

- **The Audience Owned:** Traders who want their PnL to speak instead of their pitch. Investors who want data, not deal flow.
- **The Moat:** On-chain reputation is non-transferable, non-fakeable, and cumulative. A traders score is forged, not funded.

## 03 — Brand Story

Before Arcadia, traders had two options: trade on a perp DEX and stay anonymous, or pitch investors with a deck and a prayer. Track records lived in screenshots. Capital allocation lived in trust falls.

Arcadia was built on a simple idea: **the chain already knows who can trade.** Every liquidation avoided, every profitable exit, every risk-managed position — it's all there in the ledger. The problem wasn't data. It was signal.

Arcadia extracts that signal. A traders on-chain history becomes a verified, scored, and classified reputation. Investors don't need to read a whitepaper. They read the score.

Arcadia is the place where performance speaks louder than promises. A meritocracy for capital. A proving ground for traders. A portfolio for believers.

**Arcadia. Where Performance Earns Capital.**

## 05 — Brand Voice

### Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Precise** | Data-driven, not hype-driven. Every claim has a number behind it. |
| **Confident** | Speaks with authority earned through proof. Never bragging, never doubting. |
| **Sharp** | Minimal. No filler. Every word earns its place. |
| **Aspirational** | Builds a world where performance is rewarded. | |

### Tone Spectrum

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Product UI | Minimal, functional | "Score: 912. Tier: Elite." |
| Marketing | Aspirational, sharp | "The chain knows who can trade. We just surface it." |
| Social | Confident, slightly edgy | "Your track record or your pitch. We take the first one." |
| Support | Helpful, direct | "Connect your wallet. We'll handle the rest." |
| Investor | Data-heavy, transparent | "Verified PnL. 847 trades. 68% win rate." |

### Language Rules

- **Avoid:** hype adjectives (revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive)
- **Prefer:** concrete numbers, timeframes, verifiable claims
- **Numbers:** Always use actual figures where available. "912 score" not "top-tier."
- **Action bias:** "Prove it." not "We help you prove yourself."
- **Crypto-native but not exclusive:** Use terms like "perp" and "PnL" freely, but avoid unnecessary jargon when simpler words work.

### Do Say vs Don't Say

| ✅ Do Say | ❌ Don't Say |
|-----------|--------------|
| "The chain knows who can trade." | "We're revolutionizing perp trading." |
| "Score: 912 · Elite · 847 trades" | "Industry-leading reputation system" |
| "Proven performance" | "Next-generation platform" |
| "On-chain verification" | "Blockchain-powered trust" |

## 06 — Visual Identity

### Identity Strategy Statement

Arcadia's visual identity translates the idea of **earned trust into a sensory experience.** The system lives in the tension between the cold precision of data (the trading terminal) and the aspirational warmth of a place you earn your way into (Arcadia itself). The palette is nocturnal and focused — dark voids interrupted by sharp accent light. The typography is utilitarian but elevated. The overall effect: a command center where performance is measured, not a dashboard where hype is broadcast.

### Logo Direction

**Primary direction:** Wordmark with an accompanying symbol mark.

**Character:** Precise, architectural, aspirational. The mark should feel like a **gateway or a seal** — something you earn entry to.

**Style notes:**
- Geometric, constructed lines
- Minimal — no gradients, no flourishes
- The wordmark should stand alone in caps: **ARCADIA**
- The symbol should work as a 16×16 favicon, an app icon, and a watermark

**Symbol concept:** An abstract **gateway arch** formed by two angled pillars meeting at a point — suggesting entry into a place of proven status. The negative space between them forms an **A** shape.

**What to avoid:**
- Greek columns (too literal)
- Pastoral/idyllic imagery (too on-the-nose)
- Lightning bolts, flames, or generic crypto symbols
- Shield shapes (owned by every security brand)

**3 Logo References:**
| Brand | Quality to borrow |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Stripe** | The geometric precision and reduction of the mark to its essential form |
| **Monzo** | The way a simple shape becomes a recognizable symbol through color and consistency |
| **Solana** | The upward momentum encoded in geometry — movement without clutter |

### Color Palette

```
  VOID       PANEL      LINE       FAINT      MUTED      INK
  #060609    #0E0E11   #1C1C21   #5A5A66   #8A8A99   #E6E6E6
  (base bg)  (surface)  (border)  (tertiary) (secondary)(primary)

  ACID                   SUCCESS                DANGER
  #A8FF33                #22C55E                #EF4444
  (accent — green       (positive PnL,         (negative PnL,
   lime / "proof"        verified, active)      liquidations, errors)
   glow)
```

**Palette logic:**
- **Void → Ink:** A single-axis grayscale from absolute black to near-white. No warm grays. The platform is cold, precise, data-heavy.
- **Acid:** The proof color. It glows like a verified checkmark. Used sparingly — for the logo accent, active states, and confirmation moments.
- **Success/Danger:** Strictly for PnL direction. Never decorative.

**Usage rules:**
- Acid should cover no more than 5% of any given screen
- Never place acid text on a non-dark background
- Void is the default background everywhere
- Panel surfaces should use `#0E0E11` — dark enough to contrast with void, light enough to feel layered

**Palette type:** Monochromatic with a single high-saturation accent.

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Size | Usage |
|------|----------|--------|------|-------|
| **Display** | Space Grotesk | Bold / ExtraBold | 2rem–4rem | Headlines, hero text, logo wordmark |
| **UI** | Inter | Medium / Semibold | 0.75rem–1rem | Body text, labels, buttons |
| **Mono** | JetBrains Mono | Regular / Bold | 0.625rem–0.875rem | Numbers, prices, code, terminal, scores |
| **Accent** | Instrument Serif | Regular | 1.25rem–2rem | Pull quotes, taglines, "Where Performance Earns Capital" moments |

**Type personality:**
- Space Grotesk: Geometric, sharp, confident — the voice of authority
- Inter: Utilitarian, clear, invisible — the voice of function
- JetBrains Mono: Precise, numeric, trustworthy — the voice of data
- Instrument Serif: Editorial, warm, earned — the voice of aspiration

**Usage hierarchy:**
- Headlines: Space Grotesk Bold, tracking -0.02em
- Subheads: Space Grotesk Medium, uppercase, tracking 0.08em
- Body: Inter Regular, 0.875rem
- Labels: Inter Semibold, 0.6875rem, uppercase, tracking 0.1em
- Data: JetBrains Mono, tabular-nums, tracking 0
- Tagline moments: Instrument Serif, italic optional

**Avoid:**
- Comic or display fonts
- Variable fonts with extreme width ranges
- Script or handwritten typefaces

### Imagery & Photography

**Overall aesthetic:** Dark, cinematic, architectural. Think command center lighting — dark room, one sharp light source.

**Subject matter:**
- Abstract landscapes at dusk/night
- Architectural interiors with strong geometric lines
- Macro textures: metal, glass, circuit traces
- Data visualization as art — charts, order books, heatmaps

**Color treatment:** Desaturated with a single accent color. High contrast. Deep blacks.

**What to avoid:**
- Stock photos of traders at desks
- Smiling people in offices
- Generic crypto imagery (rocket ships, moons, lambos)
- Gradients that mimic sunset or neon cyberpunk

### Iconography

**Style:** Line icons, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps, 16×16 or 20×20 base grid.

**Weight:** Regular (1.5px stroke).

**Personality:** Functional and utilitarian — icons are wayfinding, not decoration. They should feel like they belong in a terminal.

**Icon set:** Use Lucide icons (already adopted). No custom icon set needed.

### Design Principles

**Precision over flair.** Every element should feel intentional. Margins are multiples of 4. Type sizes follow a 1.25 scale. Nothing is decorative — if it doesn't carry information or afford action, remove it.

**Data as interface.** Numbers are not decoration. Scores, prices, PnL — these are the primary content. Typography and layout serve to make data readable at a glance, not to fill space.

**Dark by default.** The interface lives on dark backgrounds. Light mode should never exist — this is a terminal, not a landing page. The darkness creates focus.

**Motion with purpose.** Animations exist to convey state changes — position opened, trade confirmed, score updated — not for delight. 200ms ease-out. No parallax, no scroll-triggered reveals outside marketing.

## 07 — Brand Expressions

### Website / App

- Full dark background (void)
- Terminal-inspired layout in the trading view
- Card-based design for portfolio, vault, reputation pages
- Monospace numbers throughout
- Acid-green accents only for: verified badge, active state, confirmation

### Social Media (X, Discord)

- Profile: Logo mark on void background. No text.
- Header: Cinematic landscape with acid-green overlay mark
- Posts: Data-heavy — charts, scores, milestones. One hard number per post.
- Discord: Dark theme. Acid-green role colors. Monospace usernames.

### Pitch Deck / Presentations

- Void background, white text, one acid accent per slide
- Every slide has exactly one number that matters
- No bullet points — data points with context
- Font pairing: Space Grotesk (headlines) + JetBrains Mono (data)

### Terminal / Product UI

- The terminal is the brand
- Market data, positions, order book — all rendered in JetBrains Mono
- No decorative elements
- The "Where Performance Earns Capital" tagline appears as the terminal's loading state

## 08 — Brand Guidelines Quick Reference

### Logo

- Minimum clear space: height of the "A" in Arcadia on all sides
- Never recolor the mark — use the provided acid-green on void asset
- Never distort, rotate, or add effects
- Favicon: standalone gateway symbol mark, 16×16, acid on void

### Color Accessibility

- Ink (#E6E6E6) on Void (#060609): contrast ratio 16.4:1 — exceeds AAA
- Acid (#A8FF33) on Void (#060609): contrast ratio 8.1:1 — exceeds AA
- Never use acid on white backgrounds
- Never use muted (#8A8A99) for critical information

### Typography Safety

- Minimum body size: 0.75rem (12px)
- Minimum data size: 0.625rem (10px) — only in JetBrains Mono
- Never use all-caps for body text
- Never use Space Grotesk for data or numbers
